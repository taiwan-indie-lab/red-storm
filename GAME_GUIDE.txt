RED STORM - GAME GUIDE
=====================================
(Chinese version: 遊戲說明.txt)

[STARTING THE GAME]
  Run RedStorm_Game/RedStorm.exe
  (or play in the browser: https://taiwan-indie-lab.github.io/red-storm/)

  Language: the game picks Chinese or English from your system on first launch.
  Use the "English" / "中文" button on the main menu to switch at any time.

  To modify the game: open project.godot in the Godot 4.7 editor
  Balance values (cost, health, damage): scripts/defs.gd

[GAME MODES]
  - Skirmish: choose your country, number of enemies (1-3), difficulty, map (9 maps) and starting funds
    Start positions are random (the main opponent is always in the opposite corner); the white dot on the
    menu's map preview is your position this game - press "Regenerate Terrain" to reroll
  - Campaign: 4 story missions (Outpost Assault / Hold the Line / Drone War / Nuclear Crisis)
  - Game Mode (in the skirmish menu):
      Annihilation - destroy all enemy buildings
      Survival - the enemy has no base; waves arrive after 3 minutes, every 5th wave is a boss wave,
                 each wave repelled pays a bonus, and surviving 25 waves wins
      Control Point - the central command post opens at 2:30; hold it alone to reach 300 points and win,
                 earning +$10 per second while you control it

[BASIC CONTROLS]
  Left click           Select a unit / drag to box-select
  Double click         Select all units of that type on screen (double-click the MCV = deploy)
  Right click          Move, attack, harvest, capture with Engineers, garrison civilian buildings with infantry
  Shift + right click  Queue waypoints
  A                    Attack-move (engage enemies on the way)
  S                    Stop
  D                    Deploy the MCV / make garrisoned infantry leave a building
  E                    Select all combat units
  Q                    Select units of the same type
  Ctrl + 1-9           Assign a control group; 1-9 selects it (double-tap to jump there)
  Shift + left click on the build panel   Queue 5 units at once
  Ctrl + left click on the build panel    Repeat production (right click to cancel)
  F                    Unit ability: infantry "Dig In" (cannot move, range +1.5, damage taken -40%;
                       press again or give a move order to cancel)
                       vehicles "Smoke Grenade" (damage taken -50% for 8 seconds, 40-second cooldown)
  Z / X                Sell mode / Repair mode
  H                    Back to base
  M                    Music on / off (volume is in the Esc menu)
  Space                Jump to the latest alert (under attack / radar ping); with no alert, back to base
  Hold right button and drag   Pan the view (also arrow keys, moving the mouse to the screen edge,
                       middle-button drag, or a two-finger trackpad swipe)
  (The pause menu can switch to "left-drag pans the view"; box-select then uses Shift + drag)
  Mouse wheel          Zoom; middle-button drag pans
  + / -                Game speed; P pause; Esc menu (volume); F1 help

[SUGGESTED BUILD ORDER]
  Deploy the MCV -> Power Plant -> Refinery (comes with an Ore Truck) -> Barracks -> War Factory
  -> Radar (enables the minimap) -> Drone Station / Financial Center -> Tech Center -> Superweapon

[TECH UPGRADES] (6th tab of the build panel, "Tech"; requires Radar)
  Ballistic Plates / Precision Fire (infantry) - Composite Armor / Tungsten AP Rounds / Turbine Engine (vehicles)
  Stealth Coating (air) - Reinforced Fortifications (buildings) - Efficient Refining (harvesting +25%)
  - War Academy (new units start as veterans)
  Each can be researched once and applies to your whole army permanently; the AI researches them too

[DIFFICULTY] Easy / Normal / Hard / Brutal (AI income +75%, with earlier and more frequent mass attacks)

[TECH BUILDINGS] (neutral buildings on skirmish maps with an icon badge overhead; placed at equal
  distance from both sides' start positions)
  Capture one with an Engineer to gain its effect; lose it if an enemy Engineer takes it:
  Tech Hospital (red cross): all your infantry regain 3% health per second
  Machine Shop (gear): all your vehicles and ships regain 2% health per second
  Tech Airport (plane): grants the "Paratroopers" special ability
  The AI also sends Engineers to capture them

[BATTLEFIELD CONTRACTS] (skirmish; can be turned off in the menu)
  From about 4 minutes in, command issues timed side missions from time to time; the blue bar at the
  top left shows progress:
  Ore Truck Hunt / Decapitation Strike (the target building is marked with a red crosshair) /
  Annihilate Forces / Seize a Stronghold / Iron Wall
  Completing one pays a bounty; there is no penalty for failing

[DYNAMIC WEATHER] (skirmish; can be turned off in the menu)
  Clear skies alternate with bad weather, which depends on the map theme; the time remaining is shown
  at the top right:
  Heavy Rain: long-range weapon range -10%     Dense Fog: long-range weapon range -25%
  Thunderstorm: long-range weapon range -15%, air unit speed -25%, random lightning strikes
  Heavy Snow: ground speed -12%     Blizzard: ground speed -25%, long-range weapon range -20%, air speed -30%
  Sandstorm: long-range weapon range -25%, ground speed -10%, air speed -30%
  (Only weapons with a range of 6.5 tiles or more are affected; defensive towers are not)

[ENEMY COMMANDER PERSONALITIES] (skirmish)
  Each AI gets a random style: Balanced / Blitzer (early rushes) / Turtle (heavy defenses and artillery) /
  Air Dominator (lots of drones and aircraft) / Steel Torrent (massed heavy tanks)
  Once you build a Radar you receive intel revealing the opponent's style; the Diplomacy panel (T) shows it too

[RULE MUTATORS] ("Battlefield Options" on the right of the skirmish menu; combine freely; apply to all players)
  Fast Build (production x2) - Rich Ore (harvesting x1.5) - Nuclear Age (special weapons charge x2)
  - Glass Cannon (damage x1.6) - Veteran Forces (new units start as veterans)
  - Infantry War (infantry only) - No Superweapons - Blitzkrieg (movement speed x1.4)

[DAILY CHALLENGE] ("Daily Challenge" on the main menu)
  Each day the date fixes the country, map, opponents, difficulty and 2 rule mutators. Go for the
  fastest win; your best result of the day is recorded

[FACTIONS]
  West:    Grey Wolf Tank, Laser Tank, HIMARS, Javelin Trooper, TB2, FPV drone, Laser Tower,
           EMP Center (EMP Storm), Financial Sanctions, Starlink Recon, Destroyer, Aegis Cruiser
  East:    Heavy Tank, Bastion Heavy Tank, TOS-1A, Iskander, Lancet, Shahed-136,
           Arc Tower, Nuclear Missile Silo, Energy Cutoff, GPS Jamming, Attack Submarine, Missile Battleship

[SPECIAL WEAPONS] (they charge below the build panel once the matching building is built)
  When you fire one yourself, a 4-5 second 3D launch cinematic plays (the nuke lifting off, the storm
  forming, a missile salvo...). The battle is paused meanwhile, and the camera then centers on the target.
  Other players' launches do not interrupt you. Click or press Esc to skip; the Esc menu can turn
  cinematics off
  Nuclear Missile / EMP Storm: area-destruction strikes
  Financial Sanctions: freezes 30% of the enemy's funds and cuts their income and production for 90 seconds
  Energy Cutoff: a full enemy blackout for 60 seconds (defensive towers and radar go offline)
  Drone Swarm: launches a group of kamikaze drones
  Starlink Recon: permanently reveals a large area
  GPS Jamming: enemy drones in the area crash and defensive towers go offline for 25 seconds

[TIPS]
  - EW Vehicles and Anti-Drone Jammer Towers make nearby enemy drones crash
  - Engineers can capture oil derricks (steady income) and enemy buildings (you get their tech!)
  - Infantry garrisoned in civilian buildings shoot from cover; a gas station explodes violently when destroyed
  - Units are promoted to Veteran / Elite after 3 / 6 kills
  - With low power, production slows and defensive towers and radar shut down
